Nacho Night

A full spread — salsa, guacamole, refried beans & vegan sour cream

Step 1 · ~15 minsMild Salsa

1
Dice 3 roma tomatoes small — remove the seeds to keep the salsa from going watery. Pat dry with paper towel.
2
Finely mince ¼ white onion, 1 small jalapeño (seeded for mild heat), 1 garlic clove, and a small handful of cilantro.
3
Combine everything in a bowl. Add juice of ½ lime and a generous pinch of salt. Stir and taste — adjust salt and lime as needed.
4
Cover and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes before serving — the flavours meld beautifully with time.
✨Nice touch: Char your tomatoes and jalapeño directly on a gas burner or under the broiler for 5 minutes before dicing. It adds a subtle smoky depth that makes the salsa taste restaurant-quality.

Step 2 · ~10 minsRefried Beans

1
Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a pan over medium. Add ¼ onion, finely diced and cook 3–4 minutes until soft. Add 2 minced garlic cloves and cook 30 more seconds.
2
Add one 400g can of pinto beans (drained, but reserve the liquid). Sprinkle in ½ tsp cumin and a pinch of salt.
3
Mash the beans with a fork or potato masher as they warm, adding splashes of the reserved bean liquid (or vegetable broth) to reach your desired creamy consistency.
4
Cook on low, stirring occasionally, until thick and creamy. Taste and adjust salt. Keep warm on the lowest heat until ready to serve.
✨Nice touch: Drizzle a tiny bit of olive oil and a sprinkle of smoked paprika over the finished beans when plating. It looks stunning and adds a gentle warmth.

Step 3 · ~10 mins + soakCashew Sour Cream

1
Soak ¾ cup raw cashews in boiling water for at least 20 minutes (or overnight in cold water). Drain and rinse. Do this first if making from scratch.
2
Blend soaked cashews with 3 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp apple cider vinegar, ¼ tsp salt, and 3–4 tbsp water until completely silky smooth.
3
Taste — it should be tangy, creamy, and slightly rich. Add a drop more vinegar or salt to taste. Refrigerate until serving. It thickens as it chills.
✨Short on time? Kite Hill or Forager store-bought vegan sour cream are excellent and nearly indistinguishable from dairy. Grab one as a backup.

Step 4 · ~8 minsGuacamole

1
Halve and pit 2 ripe avocados. Scoop into a bowl. They should give easily when pressed — firm avocados won't mash well.
2
Add juice of 1 lime, ¼ tsp salt, and mash to your preferred texture — chunky is great for nachos.
3
Fold in 2 tbsp finely diced red onion, 1 tbsp cilantro, and optionally ½ a seeded jalapeño, finely minced.
4
Taste and adjust salt and lime. Make this last — guacamole browns quickly. Press cling wrap directly onto the surface if not serving immediately.
✨Nice touch: Serve the guacamole in one of the avocado skin halves. It looks beautiful and keeps it fresh longer. A little sprinkle of flaky sea salt on top elevates it instantly.
· · ·
🧀

Step 5 · ~10 minsBuilding the Nachos

1
Preheat your oven to 200°C / 400°F. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
2
Spread tortilla chips in a single layer — resist the urge to pile them high. Layering means soggy chips at the bottom. For a bigger portion, use two trays.
3
Spoon the warm refried beans in small dollops across the chips. Add your toppings: a sprinkle of jalapeño slices, perhaps some corn or black beans for texture.
4
If using a vegan shredded cheese, scatter it on now. Bake for 8–10 minutes until edges are golden and everything is warmed through.
5
Remove from the oven and immediately add the cold toppings: dollops of guacamole, salsa, and sour cream. Garnish with fresh cilantro. Serve straight from the tray.
✨Nice touch: Serve the nachos on a large wooden board brought to the table. Set small bowls of extra salsa, guac, and sour cream alongside for dipping. It feels like a feast.

Make it specialTips for a Lovely Evening

🕯️

Set the Scene

Light a couple of candles and put on some Latin jazz or mariachi playlist. Ambiance costs nothing but makes everything taste better.

🍹

Make Drinks

Virgin margaritas are easy and festive: lime juice, agave syrup, sparkling water, and salt-rimmed glasses. Or go classic with a proper margarita.

🌺

Garnish Generously

Fresh cilantro, lime wedges, and thinly sliced radishes scattered over the nachos add colour, freshness, and an impressive finishing touch.

🫙

Serve Family Style

Put each condiment in its own little bowl. Arrange them around the nacho tray like a spread. It feels abundant and inviting — a real experience.

🌶️

Offer Heat Levels

Keep a bottle of hot sauce on the table. It lets each of you customise and adds an interactive, fun element to the meal.

🎵

Timing is Everything

Prep all the dips first, then build and bake the nachos last. Everything should land at the table at the same time, warm and fresh.
